# Play the Crossfall beta

Thanks for testing Crossfall! It's a crossword where every answer you solve falls into a well like a falling-block game. Stack the words, spell the target word on the orange middle line, and find the word hiding in the pile before time runs out.

## Join (2 minutes)

You need an iPhone or iPad with iOS 16 or later.

1. On your iPhone or iPad, install **TestFlight** from the App Store. It's Apple's free beta-testing app.
2. Get your invite:
   - **Public link:** coming soon. Apple is finishing setup on our account, and the link will appear right here when it's ready.
   - **Want in now?** Email **rodlonbell16@gmail.com** with the email address of your Apple ID, with the subject "Crossfall beta". You'll get an invite email from Apple (App Store Connect). Accept it, then open TestFlight.
3. In TestFlight, tap **Crossfall**, then **Install**.
4. Open Crossfall from your home screen.

If a link ever says the beta isn't accepting testers, Apple is still reviewing the newest build (usually less than a day). Try again later.

## What to try

1. **The welcome.** Answer the 3 questions: your name and play style, how you're feeling, and your 3 free categories.
2. **The one-minute tour.** It teaches solving, dropping and the rush.
3. **Today's Daily puzzle.** It's the same puzzle for everyone each day.
4. **A few puzzles** in your free categories.
5. **Crossfall Pro.** Open a category you didn't pick, or puzzle 7 of one you did, and tap **Unlock Pro**. **In TestFlight, purchases are free test purchases. You won't be charged.** Then try **Restore purchase**.
6. **Settings (Aa).** Try Zen mode, bigger text, haptics, and light/dark.

## How to play

- **Solve:** tap a square and type the answer. Tap the clue bar to switch Across and Down.
- **Drop:** your answer becomes a falling piece. ◀ ▶ move it, ⟳ turns it, ⤓ slams it down. A full row clears.
- **Target:** spell the target word across the orange middle line for +500.
- **Rush:** when the stack gets too tall, find the hidden word in the well before the timer runs out.

- Crossfall is a game, not therapy. The optional check-in in Biblical, Psychology and Books only picks which words you see. If you're carrying something heavy, talk to someone you trust. In the US you can call or text 988 any time.
- Beta builds expire after 90 days. TestFlight updates you to new builds automatically.
