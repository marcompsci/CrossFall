# Crossfall Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 16 October 2026_

Crossfall is made by Omari Bell (Phoronomic Studios). This policy covers the Crossfall app for iPhone and iPad and its home-screen widget.

## The short version

- No ads, no analytics, no tracking, no third-party SDKs.
- No account. Crossfall never asks for your name, email or phone number.
- Your progress, journal and settings are stored **on your device**. We (the developer) never receive them.
- The app makes no network requests of its own. The only outside services it uses are Apple's: Game Center, the App Store (for Crossfall Pro), notifications and, if you choose, Location Services.

## What stays on your device

Crossfall saves these in the app's own storage on your iPhone or iPad. They are never sent to us:

- Stars, scores, stats, streaks, achievements, settings and your nickname
- Your answers to the optional check-in questions in the Biblical, Psychology and Books categories (they only choose which words appear)
- **Your journal:** reflection notes, mood check-ins and saved quotes. You can delete single entries, or everything at once (☰ › My journal › Delete all)
- Words you've solved (Word book, Review my words) and Word Trail, Ladder and Marathon progress
- Pass & Play player names you type in, kept only to fill the form next time

Deleting the app deletes all of this. The **backup code** (Settings › Back up or restore progress) is a text copy of this data that you can choose to save or share yourself. It includes your journal, so keep it private.

## Location (optional)

The Sports category can show sports legends from your area. If you tap that option, iOS asks for permission and Crossfall reads your **approximate** location once. It is matched against a list of cities built into the app, **on your device**. The coordinates are discarded straight away; only the matched city from that built-in list is saved on your device, as if you had typed it. Nothing is sent to us or anyone else. If you say no, everything else works the same. You can change this any time in iOS Settings › Privacy & Security › Location Services › Crossfall.

In App Store Connect this is listed as **Coarse Location, used for App Functionality, not linked to you, not used for tracking**.

## Game Center (optional)

If you're signed in to Game Center, Crossfall sends your scores and achievements to Apple so they appear on leaderboards, and shows your Game Center name to other players there. The Friends leaderboard asks iOS for permission to see your Game Center friends; this is used only to show their scores. Apple handles Game Center data under its own privacy policy (apple.com/legal/privacy). You can play without Game Center.

## Purchases

Crossfall Pro is a one-time purchase through Apple's App Store. Apple handles the payment; Crossfall never sees your payment details or Apple Account. The app only asks the App Store whether Pro is unlocked, and Restore Purchase checks it again.

## Notifications (optional)

If you turn on the daily reminder, iOS asks for permission and Crossfall schedules the reminder **on your device**. No notification data is sent to a server.

## Home-screen widget

The widget shows your streak and whether today's Daily is done. The app passes it that small summary through an app group on your device.

## Spoken clues

"Read clues aloud" and the Language category use the iPhone's built-in speech voices on your device.

## Children

Crossfall doesn't collect personal data from anyone, including children.

## Wellness note

Crossfall is a game, not therapy or medical advice. If you're struggling, talk to someone you trust. In the US you can call or text 988 any time.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
