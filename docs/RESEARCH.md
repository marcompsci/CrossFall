# Player research, 11 Oct 2026

This is what top crossword, word and falling-block games do, what players say is missing, and what Crossfall added as a result.

**Limits of this research:**
- Most App Store review pages and Reddit didn't load for fetching. The research leans on review aggregators, mirrored Reddit threads, help centres and articles.
- How often each complaint comes up is an estimate, not a count.

## What the best games do

- **NYT Crossword:** A gold star means no Check or Reveal, solved within 48 hours. Only gold stars keep the streak. Times are tracked per weekday. ([NYT help](https://thenewyorktimeshelpcenter.helpjuice.com/360052406391-The-New-York-Times-Crossword-Puzzle))
- **Arkadium:** score = % of squares filled without help × 100 + (time limit − seconds) × 10, plus a daily bonus. The time bonus only counts if you filled at least half yourself. ([Arkadium](https://support.arkadium.com/en/support/solutions/articles/44001739824--crossword-new-scoring))
- **Tetris guideline:**
  - 100/300/500/800 for 1–4 rows.
  - Back-to-back difficult clears ×1.5.
  - Combos +50 per step.
  - Hard drop 2 per row.
  - Hold piece and a next-piece queue.

  ([TetrisWiki](https://tetris.wiki/Scoring))
- **Tetris Effect:** letter grades, D to SS. ([TetrisWiki](https://tetris.wiki/Tetris_Effect))

## Top complaints and wishes

1. **Ads and paywalls:** too many ads, monthly ad removal, coins for hints. This is the top complaint across Wordscapes, Word Cookies, Words of Wonders, Block Blast and Tetris. ([unstar roundup](https://unstar.app/blog/wordscapes-words-with-friends-nyt-games-sudoku-word-cookies-word-games-ranked-2026))
2. **Lost streaks and progress** after sync problems, updates or reinstalls. ([Crossword+ reviews](https://justuseapp.com/en/app/1330947092/crossword/reviews))
3. **Unexplained scoring and penalties,** such as Daily Themed's hidden 20-point deductions. ([forum](https://forums.justuseapp.com/en/post/W2KYXV9D5L/themed-crossword-puzzle-keeps-taking-20-points-off-my-score-even-when-i-choose-not-to-see-wrong-answs))
4. **No next-piece preview or hold, and unfair-feeling pieces** in block games. ([Block Blast reviews](https://justuseapp.com/en/app/1617391485/block-blast/reviews))
5. **Wanting to see the finished puzzle,** and to learn what words mean.
6. **Timers that stress people out.** Players ask for relaxed or untimed modes (dyslexia and ADHD threads, Tetris training mode requests).
7. **Same puzzle as friends:** daily puzzles and share cards make comparing fair.
8. **Archives and replays** of past puzzles.
9. **Teachers want custom word lists.**
10. **Readable fonts and spacing; screen-reader support.** ([AppleVis](https://applevis.com/comment/51931))

## What Crossfall added

| Request | Crossfall 0.3 |
| --- | --- |
| Clear scoring | Line-by-line score breakdown on every results screen, plus "How scoring works" |
| Fair, motivating leaderboard | Gold solves, D–SS grades, speed chains, Tetris-style clears and combos, fall-speed multiplier, and a daily board where only your first try counts |
| Daily puzzle, streak anxiety | Daily Crossfall (same seed for everyone), streak with freezes earned every 7 days, 14-day archive, emoji share card |
| Next piece and hold | Next-3 queue and a hold slot |
| Check / reveal | Check word and an Autocheck setting; both cost the gold, which the game says up front |
| Relaxed play | Zen mode: no rush timer, no game over (scores at half) |
| Learning | Word bank of every word you've solved, plus a 10-card review that brings back the words you missed first |
| Classroom | Make a puzzle from a word list and share it as a code |
| Admire the solve | Mini crossword on the results screen |
| Lost progress | Game in progress is saved and resumes after closing the app; backup code to move progress to a new phone |
| Readability | Readable font setting, alongside bigger text, high contrast and the magnifier |
| Ads | None. No coins, no paywall. |
