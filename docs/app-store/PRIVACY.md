# Crossfall Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 11 October 2026_

Crossfall is made by Omari Bell. This policy covers the Crossfall iPhone app.

## The short version

Crossfall does not collect, sell or share your personal data. There are no ads, analytics or tracking.

## What stays on your phone

- Your stars, scores, stats and settings
- Your nickname
- Your answers to the check-in questions in the Biblical, Psychology and Books categories

These are saved only on your device. Deleting the app deletes them.

## Game Center

If you're signed in to Game Center, Crossfall sends your scores and achievements to Apple's Game Center so they appear on leaderboards. Your Game Center name is shown to other players there. Apple handles this data under its own privacy policy (apple.com/privacy). You can play Crossfall without Game Center.

## Children

Crossfall is rated 4+ and doesn't collect data from anyone, including children.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
