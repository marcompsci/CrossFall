How to play
  1. Tap a square and type. Tap the clue bar to switch Across and Down. Press and hold a clue to zoom in with the magnifying glass.
  2. A finished word drops into the well. Move it with the arrows, ⟳ to rotate, ⤓ to slam it down.
  3. Fill a whole row to clear it.
  4. Spell the target across the orange middle line, or down through it, for +500.
  5. Stack above the line? Rush: up to 2 minutes to find the hidden word. Win fast and your next rush gets shorter.
