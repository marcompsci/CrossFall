# Crossfall Support

## How to play

1. **Solve a clue.** Tap a square and type. Tap the clue bar to switch between Across and Down.
2. **Your word falls.** Move it with ◀ ▶, rotate with ⟳, drop it with ⤓.
3. **Build the target.** Spell the target word on the orange middle line for +500.
4. **Win the rush.** When the stack passes the middle line, find the hidden word before time runs out.

## Common questions

**The pieces fall too fast.** Open ☰ › Settings › Fall speed and choose Relaxed.

**The text is too small.** Turn on Bigger text in Settings, or raise the text size in your iPhone's Settings › Display & Brightness › Text Size. Crossfall follows it.

**Rankings say "Not signed in".** Sign in to Game Center in your iPhone's Settings › Game Center, then reopen Crossfall.

**Rankings say "being set up".** The leaderboards are new; try again later.

**I lost my stars.** Progress is saved on your phone. Deleting the app removes it.

**Is the check-in private?** Yes. Your answers stay on your phone and only choose which words appear. Crossfall is a game, not therapy. If you're struggling, talk to someone you trust; in the US you can call or text 988.
