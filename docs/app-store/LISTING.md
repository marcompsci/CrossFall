## Description (4,000)

```
Solve the crossword. Watch the words fall. Find what's hiding in the stack.

Crossfall crosses a classic crossword with a falling-block well. Every answer you solve drops into the well as a piece. Slide it, spin it, and stack it to spell the target word across the glowing middle line.

When the stack climbs too high, a rush begins: find the hidden word in the well before the clock runs out. Win and the top of the well clears. Miss and a junk row rises from the bottom.

NINE CATEGORIES
Swipe through game cards: Sports, Biblical, Psychology, Books, Music, Arcade, Bay City, Kitchen and Night Sky. Twenty puzzles in each, 180 in all, with stars to collect.

START FREE
Tell us your name and how you're feeling, then pick 3 free categories with 6 puzzles each. The Daily puzzle is free for everyone, every day. Crossfall Pro unlocks all 9 categories and all 180 puzzles with a single one-time purchase. No subscription.

PUZZLES THAT MEET YOU WHERE YOU ARE
Biblical, Psychology and Books begin with a short five-question check-in. Your answers choose the words, verses and quotes, built around what you need today: peace, hope, strength, rest, courage or gratitude. Your answers never leave your phone.

A RUSH THAT LEARNS
Every rush starts at two minutes. Win fast and the next one gets tighter. Struggle and it gives you time back.

PLAY YOUR WAY
• Relaxed, Normal or Fast falling speed
• Light, dark, or follow your phone
• Bigger text, high contrast and a magnifying glass for clues and tiles
• A one-minute playable tour the first time you open the game
• Hold a piece and see the next three
• Zen mode: no timer, no game over
• Check a word, or turn on Autocheck
• Readable font option
• Works with Dynamic Type and VoiceOver
• Sound effects and an optional music loop

DAILY CROSSFALL
One puzzle for everyone each day. Your first try goes on the Today leaderboard. Keep your streak going, and earn freezes that cover a missed day. Share your result with friends.

SCORING YOU CAN SEE
Every point is shown line by line: clean letters, speed chains, row clears, combos, the target, rushes and your time. Solve with no help for a gold bonus and chase an SS grade.

LEARN AS YOU PLAY
Life Moves turns chess, card games, crosswords and falling blocks into quick, playful lessons about real life: budgeting, odds, staying calm, making room for surprises and asking for help. Make the move, flip the card, take on a side quest. No lectures.
Every word you solve goes into your word bank, and quick reviews bring back the ones you're still learning. Teachers and parents can turn any spelling list into a puzzle and share it with a code.

RANKINGS AND ACHIEVEMENTS
Climb the Game Center leaderboards for today, all time, this week and best game. Unlock 9 achievements.

No ads. No account to create. No tracking.
```

## App Privacy (Data Collection)

Answer **"No, we do not collect data from this app."**

Why that's accurate:
- Stars, stats, settings and check-in answers are saved only on the device.
- Game Center scores and achievements are handled by Apple under the player's Game Center account. Apple's guidance is that developers don't declare Game Center data they don't receive or store.
- No analytics, ads, tracking or third-party SDKs.

## App Review notes

```
Crossfall is a single-player word game. No sign-in is required.

To try a full round: Play › Sports › Skate Park. Type the answer to any clue; the word falls into the Well tab. Use the arrow buttons to move it and ⤓ to drop it. When the stack crosses the orange middle line, a "rush" starts: tap the hidden word's letters in order.

Biblical, Psychology and Books start with an optional five-question check-in that chooses which words appear. The answers are stored only on the device. The check-in screen notes that the game is not therapy and lists 988 (US) for anyone who needs support.

Rankings use Game Center. If the reviewer isn't signed in to Game Center, the Ranks tab says so and the rest of the game works normally.

First launch: a 3-question welcome (name, mood, 3 free categories), then a one-minute playable tour. Free players get 6 puzzles in each of their 3 categories plus the Daily. Crossfall Pro (non-consumable, com.phoronomicstudios.crossfall.pro, $4.99) unlocks everything. To see the purchase sheet: open any category that wasn't picked, or puzzle 7 of a picked one, or Menu › Go Pro. Restore Purchase is on the same sheet.
```
