# TestFlight testers

## Internal testers (up to 100, no review)

People on your App Store Connect team.

1. App Store Connect › **Users and Access** › ➕. Add their Apple ID email, role **Marketing** or **Customer Support** is enough. They accept the email invite.
2. **Apps › Crossfall › TestFlight › Internal Testing** › ➕ to make a group (for example "Team").
3. Add the people, then under **Builds** add the newest **0.2** build. Turn on **Automatic Distribution** so new builds reach them on their own.

## External testers (up to 10,000, first build needs a short review)

Friends and players who aren't on your team.

1. **TestFlight › External Testing** › ➕, name the group (for example "Beta").
2. Add the newest 0.2 build. Fill in **What to Test** (paste the What's New list from LISTING.md) and a feedback email.
3. Submit for Beta App Review. It usually clears within a day.
4. Add testers by email, or turn on **Public Link** and share the link.

Testers install the **TestFlight** app from the App Store, then accept the invite.

## Submit for App Store review

1. **App Store › iOS App 0.2**: fill every field from [LISTING.md](LISTING.md) and upload the screenshots.
2. Under **Build**, pick the newest 0.2 build.
3. Under **Game Center**, turn it on and add the 3 leaderboards and 5 achievements (see [../GAME_CENTER_SETUP.md](../GAME_CENTER_SETUP.md)).
4. **App Privacy**: "Data Not Collected". **Age rating**: 4+. **Pricing**: Free.
5. Paste the App Review notes, then **Add for Review › Submit**.
